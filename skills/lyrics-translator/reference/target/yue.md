# Cantonese Target Lyrics (粤语目标歌词)

## Output Rules

- Produce authentic, culturally grounded Cantonese lyrics tailored to Cantopop vocal aesthetics.
- **Tone-Melody Alignment (协音)**: If translating for cover/singing, words must align with the melody's pitch contours (0430 / 0243 tonal scales) to avoid comical or confusing "tone inversion" (倒字).
- **Register Consistency**: Choose either:
  1. *Elevated/Lyrical Register (传统雅词/标准白话文书写)*: Preferred for ballads, emotional love songs, and dramatic melodies.
  2. *Colloquial Register (纯正口语白话)*: Preferred for energetic pop, rap, conversational duets, or city satire (using 嘅, 咗, 仲, 唔, 咁).
  Never mix colloquial and classical registers haphazardly without artistic purpose.
- Maintain consistent character rhythm and breath phrasing.

## Native Voice Checklist

Apply during the critique pass (common.md Step 5):

- **No Mandarinized Translationese (非粤语化语病)**: Avoid awkward calques of Mandarin slang or unnatural sentence-final particles that sound alien in spoken Cantonese.
- **Checked Tone Mastery**: Leverage Cantonese checked tones (入声: -p, -t, -k) for sharp emotional stops or dramatic climaxes.
- **Poetic Economy**: Maintain the characteristic elegance and urban melancholy of Hong Kong songwriting tradition.
- **Natural Rhyme (叶韵)**: Use traditional Cantonese rhyme categories (粤语通韵) rather than Mandarin pinyin rhymes.

## Rhythm and Length

- Aim for 4–16 Chinese characters per line; 18 is an exceptional ceiling.
- Match melodic breath groups and natural metric accents.
- For cover/singing mode, strict tonal harmony (协音) and end-rhyme take precedence.
