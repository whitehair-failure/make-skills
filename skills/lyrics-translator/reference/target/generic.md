# Generic Target Lyrics (Universal Fallback)

Used when the target language does not have a dedicated `reference/target/{target}.md` profile. Applies to any target language worldwide.

## Output Rules

- Produce idiomatic, authentic text that sounds as though originally composed by a native lyricist in the target language.
- Respect the musical breath groups: single translated lines must be singable or speakable within a single natural breath without breathless crowding.
- Preserve the emotional weight and tonal register established by the source lyric and music.
- Omit empty functional fillers or mechanical pronouns if the target language naturally drops them.
- Preserve repeated hooks and chorus refrains with consistent, punchy phrasing across the file.

## Native Voice Checklist

Apply during the critique pass (common.md Step 5):

- **No Translationese**: Check if the translated line mirrors foreign word order or clunky passive constructions.
- **Natural Rhythm & Syllable Economy**: Ensure the translated line roughly fits the musical tempo and duration implied by the source line.
- **Tone & Register**: Confirm casual songs sound conversational and literary songs retain their poetic grace.
- **No Over-Explanation**: Do not turn poetic ellipsis or subtle imagery into explanatory prose.

## Rhythm and Length

- Aim for a syllable/character count comparable to the source line (typically within ±20% of source phonetic length).
- Prioritize natural stress points and musical beat alignment over strict word-for-word density.
