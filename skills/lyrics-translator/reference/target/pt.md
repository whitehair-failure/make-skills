# Portuguese Target Lyrics

## Output Rules

- Write rhythmic, lyrical Portuguese that harmonizes with melodic pulse and vocal softness.
- Establish regional target dialect (default to Brazilian Portuguese for modern global pop/MPB/bossa, or European Portuguese if requested for fado/classical) and hold it strictly consistent.
- Utilize natural nasal rhymes (_coração / ilusão / canção_) where appropriate, as they are central to Portuguese songwriting tradition.
- Keep phrasing melodic and breezy; avoid heavy academic syntax.

## Native Voice Checklist

Apply during the critique pass (common.md Step 5):

- **No Translationese**: Avoid rigid SVO calques from English or German; let Portuguese rhythmic syncopation flow naturally.
- **Pronoun Naturalness**: In Brazilian Portuguese, use _você_ with natural proclisis (_te quero_, _me abraça_); do not force archaic _tu_ unless creating classical or fado poetry.
- **Micro-Tone Fidelity**: Capture delicate emotions (_doçura_, _leveza_, _tristeza bonita_).
- **Rhythmic Syllable Economy**: Ensure lines roll softly off the tongue without sharp, abrasive consonant collisions.

## Rhythm and Length

- Aim for 6–16 syllables per line; rarely exceed 18.
- Take advantage of vowel reduction and elision between words to align with tight musical beats.
- In cover/singing mode, aim for rich, natural end-rhymes (consoantes or toantes).
