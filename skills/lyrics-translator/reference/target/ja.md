# Japanese Target Lyrics

## Output Rules

- Write idiomatic Japanese with natural SOV order, particles, and sentence endings.
- Select pronouns, honorific distance, and final particles only when voice and relationship are established by the lyric.
- Match the song's register: casual lyrics take colloquial endings (だ / ちゃう / たい), not stiff written forms (書き言葉・硬い漢語).
- Render foreign names and loanwords consistently; use Japanese wording for meaning rather than mechanically copying source syntax.
- Preserve repeated hooks and parallelism with comparable mora rhythm.

## Rhythm and Length

- Prefer concise lines of 20 mora or fewer. Expand up to 35 mora only when the source line and melody clearly support it; never exceed 40.
- Match strong beats and breath groups, not literal syllable counts.
- Do not fill a short source line with explanatory particles or modifiers.
