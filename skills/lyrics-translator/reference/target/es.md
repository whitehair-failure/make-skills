# Spanish Target Lyrics

## Output Rules

- Write vibrant, natural Spanish with fluid rhythmic cadences and natural metric accentuation.
- Leverage the pro-drop nature of Spanish: omit unnecessary subject pronouns (*yo*, *tú*) unless rhythmic meter or dramatic contrast requires them.
- Use natural rhythmic stress: Spanish lyrics naturally emphasize penultimate-syllable stress (*palabras llanas* or *graves*), which comfortably matches feminine musical downbeats.
- Match register: decide whether the song calls for informal Latin American Spanish (*tú/ustedes*) or Iberian European Spanish (*tú/vosotros*) and maintain consistency.

## Native Voice Checklist

Apply during the critique pass (common.md Step 5):

- **No Translationese**: Avoid calquing English/Germanic passives (*fue amado por mí* → prefer *yo lo amaba* or *lo quise tanto*).
- **Sinalefa Optimization**: When aiming for singability, utilize natural vowel merging (*sinalefa*) to fit syllables smoothly into tight rhythmic bars.
- **Natural Word Order**: Favor emotional topic placement over rigid SVO structures.
- **Idiomatic Slang & Passion**: Avoid bookish or stiff academic phrasing; Spanish song lyrics thrive on raw emotional immediacy (*el alma*, *el corazón*, *la piel*).

## Rhythm and Length

- Aim for 6–16 pronounced syllables per line; rarely exceed 18.
- Account for *sinalefa* when calculating vocal singability.
- In cover/singing mode, aim for assonant (*asonante*) or consonant (*consonante*) rhymes at phrase endings.
