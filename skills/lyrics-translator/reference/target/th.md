# Thai Target Lyrics

## Output Rules

- Write gentle, melodious Thai with natural phrasing, appropriate post-positioned modifiers, and sweet lyric resonance.
- Choose pronouns that accurately capture the singer's gender and interpersonal intimacy (_ฉัน-เธอ_, _เรา-เธอ_, _พี่-น้อง_).
- Use mood particles (_นะ_, _จ๊ะ_, _หรอก_) gracefully to soften rhythm and match vocal breathing points.
- Preserve SVO word order with modifiers following head nouns/verbs naturally.

## Native Voice Checklist

Apply during the critique pass (common.md Step 5):

- **No Translationese**: Avoid calquing English passive voice (_ถูก/โดน_ should only be used for unpleasant/adverse events, never for neutral or positive occurrences like _being loved_).
- **Smooth Word Segmentation**: Format text with clear phrase spaces at natural musical rests.
- **Particle Harmony**: Ensure sentence-final particles match the singer's persona without sounding overly stiff or childish.
- **Poetic Diction**: Balance conversational clarity with delicate emotional lyricism.

## Rhythm and Length

- Aim for 6–15 syllables per line; rarely exceed 18.
- Group syllables to match the melodic downbeats and breath groups.
- In cover/singing mode, utilize Thai internal and external rhyme schemes (_สัมผัสใน_, _สัมผัสนอก_) where possible.
