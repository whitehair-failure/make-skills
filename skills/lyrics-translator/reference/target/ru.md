# Russian Target Lyrics

## Output Rules

- Write expressive, melodically resonant Russian with precise stress placement and emotional depth.
- Align word stress with musical downbeats: Russian listeners immediately notice displaced stresses (_неправильное ударение_).
- Exploit Russian case flexibility to place the emotional core or rhyming word at the natural acoustic peak of the line.
- Maintain register: distinguish between formal/literary address (_Вы_) and intimate/personal address (_ты_).

## Native Voice Checklist

Apply during the critique pass (common.md Step 5):

- **No Translationese**: Avoid calquing English auxiliary verbs (_я буду любить_ when a simple perfective _полюблю_ or imperfective _люблю_ is far more natural).
- **No Copula Overuse**: Never insert unnatural verbs where Russian naturally relies on direct juxtaposition (_Он — мой герой_, not _Он является моим героем_).
- **Emotional Authenticity**: Choose evocative Russian lyric vocabulary (_тоска_, _боль_, _свет_, _тишина_) rather than flat journalistic synonyms.
- **Rhythmic Metric Flow**: Check against standard Russian poetic meters (Iambic, Trochaic, Anapestic, Amphibrachic, Dactylic) where applicable.

## Rhythm and Length

- Aim for 5–14 syllables per line; rarely exceed 16.
- Leave room for multi-syllabic Russian words; avoid overcrowding rapid tempos.
- In cover/singing mode, aim for alternating masculine (final stressed syllable) and feminine (penultimate stressed syllable) rhymes.
