# Russian Target Lyrics

## Output Rules

- Write expressive, melodically resonant Russian with precise stress placement and emotional depth.
- Align word stress with musical downbeats: Russian listeners immediately notice displaced stresses (*неправильное ударение*).
- Exploit Russian case flexibility to place the emotional core or rhyming word at the natural acoustic peak of the line.
- Maintain register: distinguish between formal/literary address (*Вы*) and intimate/personal address (*ты*).

## Native Voice Checklist

Apply during the critique pass (common.md Step 5):

- **No Translationese**: Avoid calquing English auxiliary verbs (*я буду любить* when a simple perfective *полюблю* or imperfective *люблю* is far more natural).
- **No Copula Overuse**: Never insert unnatural verbs where Russian naturally relies on direct juxtaposition (*Он — мой герой*, not *Он является моим героем*).
- **Emotional Authenticity**: Choose evocative Russian lyric vocabulary (*тоска*, *боль*, *свет*, *тишина*) rather than flat journalistic synonyms.
- **Rhythmic Metric Flow**: Check against standard Russian poetic meters (Iambic, Trochaic, Anapestic, Amphibrachic, Dactylic) where applicable.

## Rhythm and Length

- Aim for 5–14 syllables per line; rarely exceed 16.
- Leave room for multi-syllabic Russian words; avoid overcrowding rapid tempos.
- In cover/singing mode, aim for alternating masculine (final stressed syllable) and feminine (penultimate stressed syllable) rhymes.
