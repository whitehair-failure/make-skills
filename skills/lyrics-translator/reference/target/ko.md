# Korean Target Lyrics

## Output Rules

- Write idiomatic Korean with appropriate particles, connective endings, and speech level.
- Carry source honorifics and intimacy as Korean register, not literal grammatical labels.
- Match speech level to the song's voice: casual lyrics take 반말 endings, not literary or formal ones.
- Render culture-specific terms and sound words by their contextual effect; avoid gratuitous transliteration.
- Keep repeated hooks and parallel phrases balanced in syllable count and register.

## Rhythm and Length

- Aim for 4–16 Hangul syllables per line; never exceed 18.
- Match strong beats and breath groups rather than literal source syllable counts.
- Keep short hooks short and avoid padding with extra endings.
