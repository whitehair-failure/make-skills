# Vietnamese Target Lyrics

## Output Rules

- Write lyrical, poignant Vietnamese with natural tonal melody and balanced word choices.
- Establish the relationship pronouns (*anh–em*, *tôi–bạn*, *mình–người*) early and adhere to them strictly throughout the song.
- Match register: choose pure Vietnamese (*từ thuần Việt*) for intimate, gentle emotions, and Sino-Vietnamese (*từ Hán Việt*) for epic, historic, or dramatic themes.
- Preserve parallel structures and couplet symmetry where the melody offers balanced musical phrases.

## Native Voice Checklist

Apply during the critique pass (common.md Step 5):

- **No Translationese**: Avoid translating passive structures mechanically with *bị* (negative) or *được* (positive) unless true passive voice is intended.
- **Tonal Harmony (Bằng/Trắc)**: When possible, align tone contours with the rising or falling notes of the melody.
- **Reduplication for Texture**: Use natural reduplicative words (*từ láy*) to convey emotional atmosphere without adding extra explanatory syllables.
- **No Clunky Connectives**: Omit stiff grammatical connectors (*bởi vì*, *do đó*) when the emotional flow already implies causality.

## Rhythm and Length

- Aim for 4–14 words/syllables per line; rarely exceed 16.
- Since every Vietnamese word is one syllable, character counts remain compact while semantic density is high.
- In cover/singing mode, respect end-rhymes and internal vowel assonance (*vần chân*, *vần lưng*).
